# MyAquaTrax

Home Assistant integration for the MyAquaTrax water usage portal.

## Features

- Track water usage in **Gallons**, **CCF/HCF**, and **Dollars**
- Compare usage against previous year, utility average, and zip code average
- Supports both standard email/password and SSO (Account + Email + Street) logins
- Auto-discovers water meters after login
- Energy Dashboard compatible sensors

## Supported Utilities

Any water district that uses the MyAquaTrax portal, including:

- City of Newport Beach
- City of American Canyon
- City of Morgan Hill
- City of Gilroy
- City of Goodyear
- City of La Marque
- City of White Plains
- City of San Jose
- Rowland Water District
- Fairview City
- And many more
